Bio

I am a fourth-year undergraduate student in Software Engineering at Sichuan University. My research focuses on efficient generation with diffusion models and long video generation.

I am particularly interested in understanding and accelerating the feature dynamics of Diffusion Transformers, with the goal of making high-quality image and video generation substantially more efficient.
