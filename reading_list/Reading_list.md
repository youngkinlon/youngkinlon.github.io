## Why keep a reading list?

In the age of AI, I’ve noticed that I have less patience for reading papers closely. But the devil is in the details. I keep this list to get back into the habit of reading carefully and to develop my research taste.

I look for papers my peers recommend, including work outside my field; recent papers on ideas I think are about to take off; and papers that connect directly to my own research.
